The game's galaxy-map markers, used as page icons (2026-10-15): destination.svg (every button that targets a system
in the galaxy map), fleet-carrier.svg and squadron-carrier.svg (carriers), titan.svg (a Titan among the signals worth a
detour), waypoint.svg (the route line's next stop), engineer.svg and mission.svg (the CMDR tab's Engineering and
Missions). The page draws each as a CSS mask in the text's own colour (page.css, .mk).
Unofficial, non-commercial fan use: the markers' designs belong to Frontier Developments plc, and ED Outrider is not
affiliated with Frontier. These files are not under the project's GPL: they keep the terms given here.

Source
  Elite Dangerous Almanac's galaxy-map markers (assets/galaxy-map/<symbol>.svg: hand-drawn 64 x 64 vector recreations
  of the in-game markers, each painted with currentColor), https://github.com/Elite-Dangerous-Almanac/Almanac-Core at
  commit 5da3bfca20aba4bb39332628e220ea2acb5dbcd9 (2026-09-26), the commit the ship pictures (static/ships/) come
  from. Copied byte for byte, seven of its sixteen markers; nothing else of Almanac's is used here.

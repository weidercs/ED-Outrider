Ship pictures shown in the CMDR tab's Inventory on a hover (a tap on the tablet): <symbol>.webp, one per hull, named
by the journal's ship symbol (krait_light.webp, explorer_nx.webp...).
Unofficial, non-commercial fan use: the ships' designs belong to Frontier Developments plc, and ED Outrider is not
affiliated with Frontier. These files are not under the project's GPL: they keep the terms given here.

Source
  Rendered from Elite Dangerous Almanac's ship illustrations (assets/ships/<Symbol>/illustration.svg), drawn by that
  project from each ship's 3D model: https://github.com/Elite-Dangerous-Almanac/Almanac-Core at commit
  5da3bfca20aba4bb39332628e220ea2acb5dbcd9 (2026-09-26). Each SVG was drawn at 600 x 400 on a transparent background
  and saved as WebP by scripts/build_ship_images.py; nothing else of Almanac's is used (none of its data files).
